# Abhimanyu - Character Sheet

## Appearance

- Handsome young warrior prince, 16 years old
- Long flowing dark hair, golden skin
- Golden armor with sun motifs, dark blue cape
- Carries the legendary bow with crescent arrow marks
- Determined, noble bearing with a warm smile

## Personality

- Fearless, brilliant, compassionate
- Learned the Chakravyuh from his father Arjuna (but not the exit)
- Fights for honor, refuses to surrender

## Prompt style

When writing prompts for video generation, prefix with:
`High-quality anime style, vibrant cel shading, dramatic lighting, `
and reference `assets/characters/abhimanyu/reference.png` as the first frame.
