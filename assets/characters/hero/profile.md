# Hero - Character Sheet

## Appearance

- Tall, athletic build, late 20s
- Dark hooded cloak, silver clasp
- Short dark hair, faint scar over left eyebrow
- Carries a worn leather satchel

## Personality

- Stoic, observant, dry humor
- Protects the innocent, distrusts authority

## Prompt style

When writing prompts for video generation, prefix with:
`Cinematic film still, photorealistic, golden hour lighting, `
and reference `assets/characters/hero/reference.png` as the first frame.
