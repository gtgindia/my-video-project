# Episode 001 - Working title

## Scene 1 - The Hero's Arrival

Character: hero

A lone figure walks out of the morning mist toward the city gates.
The hero, confident and steady, adjusts their cloak as golden sunlight
breaks through the clouds.

## Scene 2 - Confrontation

Character: villain

Inside a dark hall, the villain turns from the throne. Shadows crawl
across the floor as they address an unseen visitor.

## Scene 3 - The Chase

Character: hero

The hero sprints across rooftops, leaping between chimneys while guards
give chase below. The city sprawls beneath a crimson sunset.
